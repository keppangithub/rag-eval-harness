## THE CLIENT

Alfa Laval is a global industrial leader operating in over 100 countries. To maintain this position, fast and reliable access to information is essential, from troubleshooting and proposals to specification checks and service history.

As the product portfolio grew and digitalization accelerated, information became fragmented across systems and tools. This slowed work and made it harder to deliver a consistent customer experience.

To address this, Alfa Laval set a clear goal: to democratize access to information. The result is **ALVA,** a single intelligent entry point for fast, consistent, and secure access.

*“With ALVA, our ambition was clear: make information accessible to every employee, partner, and customer at the moment they need it. What impressed me most was how closely the outcome matched that vision. We now have a platform that truly democratizes knowledge and strengthens the way we operate globally.”*

**Hans Troiza, CTO, Alfa Laval**

## THE CHALLENGE

Alfa Laval’s information landscape was vast and complex, shaped by decades of growth. As the business expanded, knowledge spread across multiple systems and formats, making it harder to access quickly. This created daily friction, more time spent searching, increased internal support requests, and heavier onboarding efforts. 

To address this, Alfa Laval combined its deep industry expertise and innovation capacity with our partnership. Together, we set out to build a secure, scalable, always-available solution trusted by 25,000 users, and launch it globally in just 10 weeks. 

The shared vision: a secure, AI-agentic platform to unify information, accelerate work, empower teams and reduce support dependency, delivered fast without compromising quality or trust.

*"We were facing a complex challenge with very limited time, but that only strengthened our creativity and drive. Together with Alfa Laval, we turned big requirements into concrete solutions faster than any of us thought possible. It proved that with the right partnership, even the toughest challenges can be transformed into real results."*

**Amina Ovčina, Lead Developer – Reeinvent**

Together with Alfa Laval, the Trident partnership designed and built Alva as a long-term platform – not a one-off project.

* **Reeinvent** Led product and platform development: shaping the user experience, architecture, and features to make Alva genuinely useful rather than “just another dashboard”.
* **System Verification** contributed AI, QA and testing expertise. The AI knowledge enabled the **Personal Agent**, a chatbot that supports the employees with tasks like research, image generation, and data analysis. QA and testing ensure Alva remains stable, reliable, and trustworthy.
* **CodeScene** Added advanced code analysis, transforming codebases into actionable insights on technical debt, hotspots, and risk, giving teams data-driven signals on where to focus next.

## THE RESULT

The result is an AI agentic platform. In less than 10 weeks, Alva went from concept to a global launch for 25,000 users, and it now expands with role-based agents and new use cases as Alfa Laval’s needs grow.

* **One shared platform for information**
* **Time savings across teams and roles**
* **Secure and healthy platform that scales**

By combining Alfa Laval’s domain expertise with the technical capabilities of Reeinvent, System Verification and CodeScene, we moved from concept to worldwide rollout at remarkable speed. The result is a robust, enterprise-ready solution that exceeded expectations.” 
**Fredrik Nilsson, Team Manager – Alfa Laval***
