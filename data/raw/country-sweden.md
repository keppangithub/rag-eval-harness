# SWEDEN

Welcome to our operations in Sweden! We have offices in several of the country's largest cities – Malmö, Stockholm and Göteborg – where we, together with our teams, create solutions that make a real difference. Here, we combine local presence with international strength to offer high-quality services within software quality assurance.

# ABOUT SWEDEN

We’ve had a strong presence in Sweden since 2002, with our headquarters located in Malmö, where much of our operations took shape. Over time, we’ve expanded to other cities in response to growing customer needs and team development. Today, we are around 150 employees in Sweden, working across our offices. Each office is part of our whole but also has its own unique character and specialization. We work closely across office borders and with support from our international colleagues. 

Our Swedish organization is built around clear areas of responsibility and close leadership, with regional managers who know their teams and the market well. This allows us to be agile, responsive, and long-term in our efforts. 

## With us, you get more than just a job

You become part of a connected network of specialists where knowledge is shared, ideas are tested, and innovation is encouraged. In Sweden, we offer: 

* Opportunities to work with both national and international clients

* Clear career paths and investments in professional development

* Flexible working models with a healthy balance between remote and on-site work

* An inclusive company culture where everyone has a voice

* Strong networks within testing, automation, agile methodologies and AI

## Offices in Sweden

## Skåne

Skåne consists of our headquarter in Malmö and is our biggest region. We are in total around 90 employees in the region. The regional managers for Skåne are Andreas Axelsson and Karolina Mileros. We have over 60 clients in the region across various industries and of different sizes. Our clients range from industrial companies, energy providers, public transportation, healthcare, e-commerce, embedded systems, to logistics. Our first hired consultant at the company, Thomas Walentin, is still working with us after all these years.  

Our office is located at the 28th & 29th floor in The Point in Hyllie. The address is Hyllie Stationstorg 31. As the highest building in the area, we have the luxury having an amazing view 360 degrees. We share our office space with the other two companies in our ecosystem, Reeinvent and CodeScene. Which we collaborate a lot with in both strategy and delivery.

## Göteborg

The Göteborg office was our second established office, opened in 2007. Today we are located at Lindholmen, Pumpgatan 1, in the coworking space United Spaces. Igor Simunovic, the regional manager for the Göteborg region, oversees the operations here. Our first consultant, Freddy Gustafsson, is still with us since 2007, and today we have around 28 consultants in the region, with plans to continue expanding, given the region's maturity and strong market potential.  

We have a diverse team, with men and women aged 30 to 60, covering all areas of QA—from requirements and manual testing to test automation. We work with a wide range of clients in our region within the defense, automotive, recycling, banking industry, and public sector.

Contact us

## Stockholm

Our Stockholm office opened in 2008 and is today located at Helio Klarakvarteren coworking space at Gamla Brogatan 32. We have a total of 22 employees in the region. The regional manager in Stockholm is Madeleine Erlandsson.

Our customers in the region are mostly within the finance sector but are also in sectors like energy, healthcare, and telecom. We see big potential in the region and are now focusing on the defense industry as well.

Something that really is worth highlighting is that Stockholm’s first consultant, Freddie Söderkvist, is still a part of the System Verification. We are a tight group of people here in Stockholm that love to hang out with each other and learn from each other.
