## Why nearshoring?

We offer nearshore services from Sarajevo and Warsaw, enabling you to expand your team or outsource entire functions. You’ll get dedicated, skilled professionals with the right attitude. Bosnia and Poland share similar business ethics with Sweden, ensuring stable cooperation. ## Work ethics and moral values

Bosnia, Polish and Sweden share striking similarities in work ethics and moral values. Both cultures prioritize responsibility, integrity, loyalty, and ethical business practices. 

In all three countries, there is a strong emphasis on maintaining a sense of duty towards one’s work and colleagues, as well as a commitment to upholding high standards of professionalism.

This shared ethos fosters stable cooperation and ensures that teams from both nations can collaborate effectively and harmoniously on various projects.
