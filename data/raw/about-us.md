## Who are we?

Our journey started in 2002 in Skåne, Sweden, as the country’s first quality assurance company. While our headquarters remain in Skåne, we’ve since grown to eleven offices across five countries.

Though much has evolved, our core values remain unchanged: we care about our colleagues, support young talent, and truly enjoy helping our clients. We’re proud of our diverse team and the broad perspective it brings - but what unites us all is a shared passion for quality. We believe in collaboration, long-term thinking, and building sustainable success together.

## Our History

**2002** – In 2002, System Verification is founded as Sweden’s first company dedicated exclusively to quality assurance. 

**2004** – Hamdija Jusufagic becomes the sole owner of the company and expands the company’s services to cover all aspects of software quality assurance. A lot of new talent is recruited, and the company grows quickly. 

**2008-2009** – Company wins Dagens Industri’s Gasell Award in both 2008 and 2009 and is named the fastest-growing business in Skåne in 2009. That same year, our owner, Hamdija Jusufagic, is named Årets Manliga Stjärnskott by Ernst & Young.

**2012** – The company keeps growing rapidly and in 2012, we have 185 employees working in Malmö, Gothenburg, and Stockholm.

**2013** – We open our first office outside of Sweden, our Sarajevo office, and Hamdija Jusufagic is named Business Leader of the Year in Sweden. 

**2014-2019** – Our company continues to expand as we open two offices in Germany – Frankfurt and Bonn, and our existing teams continue to grow.  

**2020** – Despite global difficulties, our company continues to expand and we open an office in Warsaw.  

**2022-2023** – We celebrate 20 years as a company, open a brand new office in Hamburg, and recruit more than 70 new colleagues.

What is test automation and why is it important?

Test automation means automating testing processes to increase efficiency, reduce errors, and improve change confidence. It’s especially critical in complex systems with frequent releases.

Which tools do you use for test automation?

We strive to be tool-agnostic and aim to use the technologies that best align with each customer’s architecture and workflow. Our consultants have broad expertise across languages like C#, Java, Python and JavaScript/TypeScript, and can implement automation for backend, API, frontend, and full end-to-end flows. We work with both modern and traditional automation frameworks and platforms, such as Playwright, Selenium, Cypress, Ranorex, and the CI/CD systems that support your delivery pipeline.

What does a typical test automation project look like?

We set up frameworks, define requirements, document processes, and test functionality. For example, our experts have built frameworks using C# and Playwright.

What is quality assurance in software development?

Quality assurance is a structured process to ensure software meets requirements and is secure, stable, and user-friendly. We work with test strategy, performance testing, security testing, and continuous testing.

**Which KPIs do you use to measure QA?**

**Examples of KPIs we use:** 

* **Test coverage** – ensuring critical functionality is validated.
* **Bug rate** – tracking defects across development and production.
* **Retest time** – measuring efficiency in fixing and verifying issues.
* **Delivery precision** – assessing how closely releases meet planned timelines.
* **Reported issues/incidents from production or end users** – monitoring real-world impact.
* **User feedback surveys** – capturing satisfaction and usability insights.
* **Hotfix/Rollout time** – evaluating responsiveness to urgent fixes and deployments.
